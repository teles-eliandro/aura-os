Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Who we are

AURA-OS ("we", "us", "our") is an independent product curation and content publishing project. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit our website or interact with our content on social platforms, and what rights you have over it.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at digitalizeltda@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the project.

  • Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may log standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and reliability purposes.
  • Analytics. If aggregate analytics are enabled, they are used only to understand overall traffic volume. We do not build personal profiles of visitors.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and we do not ask for sensitive personal data.

3. How we use information

  • To answer messages you send us.
  • To keep our website secure and working correctly.
  • To understand which content is useful, in aggregate.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Affiliate links and third parties

Some links on our site and in our published content are affiliate links. When you click one, the merchant or affiliate network may set cookies or receive limited technical information to attribute the referral. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any merchant before purchasing.

5. Social platforms

We publish content on social platforms, including Pinterest. When you view or interact with our content there, the platform's own privacy policy applies. We receive only the aggregate, platform-provided information that any public account holder can see.

6. Cookies

Our website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Cookies that appear when you click an affiliate link are set by the destination merchant or network, and you can control them through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any reasonable follow-up. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider for a short, standard period and then removed.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to certain processing, or to request that we restrict it. To exercise any of these rights, email us and we will respond within a reasonable period.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited information we handle. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
